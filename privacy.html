Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

Excursion Explorer is a portfolio and educational travel website created to help users explore destinations, weather information, country details, maps, and travel inspiration.

Information We Collect

This website does not collect, store, or sell personal information from visitors.

Search and Destination Data

When you search for a destination, the website may use third-party APIs to retrieve travel-related information such as weather, local time, country data, and map details.

Third-Party Services

Excursion Explorer may use external services such as weather APIs, country information APIs, map providers, and icon or font libraries. These services may process technical information according to their own privacy policies.

Cookies

This website does not intentionally use cookies for tracking or advertising purposes.

Data Storage

No personal data is stored by Excursion Explorer. If a theme preference is saved in the browser later, it will only be stored locally on the user's device.

Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, please use the contact page.
